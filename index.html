Got Questions

Frequently asked

What should I wear and bring to my first class?

Comfortable athletic clothing and clean trainers suitable for indoor lifting. Bring a water bottle — lockers, towels and showers are provided free.

Are the classes beginner friendly?

Yes. Every exercise is scaled to your current level, so you get a safe, effective session regardless of experience.

What is the membership cancellation policy?

Monthly memberships require 30 days' notice to cancel. Annual memberships are non-refundable but can be paused for up to 60 days a year for medical or travel reasons.

Do you provide nutrition guidance?

Yes — Unlimited and Annual members get quarterly body composition scans and baseline macro guidance from our in-house coaches.

How do I book my free trial class?

Fill out the trial booking form above with your preferred class and date. Our team confirms your reservation within two hours.
