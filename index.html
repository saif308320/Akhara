Got Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear and bring to my first class?

Wear comfortable athletic clothing and clean trainers suitable for indoor lifting and movement. Bring a water bottle; we provide top-tier lockers, towels, and showers free of charge.

Are the classes beginner friendly?

Absolutely. Our expert coaches scale every exercise according to your current fitness level, ensuring you get a safe, effective workout regardless of your experience.

What is the membership cancellation policy?

Monthly memberships require a standard 30-day notice period to cancel. Annual memberships are non-refundable but can be paused for medical or travel reasons up to 60 days per year.

Do you provide nutritional guidance?

Yes! All unlimited and annual membership tiers include quarterly body composition scans and baseline nutritional macro recommendations from our certified in-house coaches.

How do I book my free trial class?

Simply fill out the free trial booking form on this page with your preferred class and date. Our team will contact you within 2 hours to confirm your reservation.
